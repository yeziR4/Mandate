# Ambassador programme — pack

Everything needed to run the recruitment sprint: the offer, the form, the DMs, and the rules that
keep every wallet countable.

---

## The offer, in one line

**Mandate Research Ambassador** — get paid to attack AI treasury agents, write up what you find, and
bring two people who try it themselves. Paid per deliverable, in fiat or however you prefer.

Why it's real work, not a shill: reading a one-page policy for loopholes, constructing a prompt that
makes an agent authorise the wrong payment, and documenting it reproducibly is exactly what
red-team and agent-security teams hire for. Every ambassador leaves with a public, numbered
certificate on Metis Andromeda that proves they did it.

## What counts as done

| Deliverable | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Report** | Three attempts against three different agents. What you said, what the agent did, screenshots of the verdict. One page is enough. |
| **Public post** | Your certificate or a screenshot of a break, tagging `@MetisL2` and `#MetisRise` and linking the site. |
| **Two referrals** | Two people who sign in with their **own** account and make at least one attempt. You introduce them; they play on their own. |

Pay is per accepted deliverable, agreed individually. Never per transaction, never per break, never
sent to the wallet you play from.

## The rules that keep everyone countable

These come from Metis and are non-negotiable:

1. **Your own wallet, your own gas.** We do not send you METIS or m.USDC to play with. A wallet that
   receives project money before it uses the product does not count.
2. **Payment goes anywhere except the playing wallet** — bank, PayPal, UPI, or a different address you
   nominate and never play from.
3. **Sign in with email (Privy)** if you can — it creates a wallet for you and is one of the
   legitimacy signals Metis verifies at month-end, alongside wallet age and prior Metis history.
   X login is disabled until its OAuth app is fixed; use email or connect your own wallet.
4. **Three transactions per person is the target** — three attempts, spaced a minute apart. That is
   what turns a visitor into a counted user.

## Form fields

Short enough to fill on a phone:

1. Name or handle
2. X handle (this is the legitimacy signal — required)
3. Wallet address you will play from (we never fund it; it is so you can find your certificate)
4. Do you already hold METIS for gas? (yes / no / need help bridging)
5. Which agents did you attack, and what did you try?
6. Screenshot or transcript link
7. Will you post it publicly? (yes / later)
8. Who referred you? (optional)

## What to tell people who ask "where's the money"

The truth, briefly: the bounty is **recorded on the certificate at the moment of the break** and
settled after review at the end of the month — by ticket, to the wallet or payout method you choose.
Winners are listed publicly with their certificate number, so the record is checkable.

## Tracking

Keep one sheet: handle · wallet · date joined · attempts · certificate # · posted (link) · referrals ·
paid (reference). It is also the evidence pack if Metis asks about any wallet during the 7-day review
window.
